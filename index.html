AppBell

Your in-app sales, live on your iPhone

AppBell rings for every in-app purchase, subscription renewal and refund of your apps, with a minimal dashboard and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. For iPhone and iPad.

No SDK. No code. No account. Paste one URL in App Store Connect and you're done. It works right away for every app, including versions already on the App Store.

  • Purchases, new subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, billing issues, refunds, Family Sharing and offer codes.
  • Today, 7 days and 30 days: customer prices including tax, refunds deducted, converted to your currency.
  • Private by design: your sales pass through the AppBell server without being stored. All data stays on your iPhone.
  • Apple sends these notifications for in-app purchases and subscriptions; paid-app downloads are not included.
